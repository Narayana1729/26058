Autonomous Closed-Loop Principle

High-frequency sound (450 kHz / 3.2 kHz test) yields millimeter resolution but scatters violently in suspended mud. When the TSW-20 sensor detects rising turbidity, the STM32 autonomously shifts carrier frequency down to 250 kHz or 100 kHz (600 Hz) within 30 ms, cutting acoustic absorption loss by up to 80% to preserve vehicle navigation and target detection.
